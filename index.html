Frequently Asked Questions

Can I buy a used car from China?

Used vehicles are available in the Chinese market. Always check the vehicle condition, history and purchase requirements.

Should I check the inspection information?

Yes. Review available inspection reports, vehicle photos, mileage and other condition information before buying.

Is the advertised price the final cost?

Not necessarily. Additional charges may apply depending on shipping, export, customs and your destination country.

Can international buyers purchase cars?

International purchases may be possible, but requirements depend on the destination country and applicable regulations.

Does this website sell cars?

No. This website is an independent informational guide and does not directly sell or own vehicles.
